# Confidence Calibration

Properly calibrating confidence is essential for risk management. Never overstate the certainty of an outcome.

- **High Confidence (>0.8)**: Requires absolute confluence. Official corporate filing + strong price confirmation (breakout) + 3+ reliable media sources corroborating + FII/DII buying support.
- **Medium Confidence (0.5-0.8)**: Requires a verified news event + mild price confirmation + at least 2 independent sources.
- **Low Confidence (<0.5)**: Social media hype only, OR a single media source without official filing, OR significant news with no price confirmation whatsoever.
- **Social Media Cap**: Never mark confidence > 0.85 for signals derived primarily from social media or unverified chat rooms, regardless of how bullish the text appears.
