# Signal Reasoning

When reasoning about market signals, always remember that a high-probability signal is a convergence of factors. A standalone news piece or a standalone technical breakout is often a trap.

- **Convergence**: A true signal equals News Catalyst + Price Confirmation + Volume Validation. If any leg of this stool is missing, the signal is weak.
- **Three-Source Rule**: Prefer signals corroborated by 3 or more independent sources. An NSE filing backed by a Reuters article and strong DII buying is a tier-1 signal. A single tweet is noise.
- **Time Decay**: Markets price in information rapidly. News signals older than 24 hours lose significant relevance for intraday trading. Signals older than 72 hours lose relevance for short-term swing trading. Do not recommend trades based on stale catalysts.
- **Price Context**: Never recommend a trade or signal without checking the current price context. Great news on a stock that is already up 50% in a month might be a "sell the news" event. Great news on a stock at major support is a high-conviction buy.
