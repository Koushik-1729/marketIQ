# Indian Market Context

When operating in the Indian market, keep these structural realities in mind:

- **Trading Hours**: NSE/BSE cash market hours are 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST. 
- **Pre-market Window**: The critical analysis window for overnight news is 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM IST.
- **Institutional Drivers**:
  - **FII** = Foreign Institutional Investor. They have a high impact on large caps and dictate broad market direction.
  - **DII** = Domestic Institutional Investor (Mutual Funds, LIC). They often act as a stabilising force, buying when FIIs sell.
  - FII buying + DII buying on the same day = strongest bullish market signal.
- **Volatility**: India VIX interpretation: <15 is calm, 15-25 is moderate volatility, >25 is high volatility / fear.
- **Sectors**: Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, and Nifty Pharma are the most heavily traded and followed sectoral indices.
- **Delivery**: Delivery percentage matters immensely in India. High delivery (>60%) on a volume spike indicates genuine accumulation (buying for the portfolio), whereas high volume with low delivery is just intraday speculation.
