# Counter-Bias Reasoning

The market is designed to deceive. You must actively hunt for contradictions to protect capital.

- **Check Price Action**: Does the price action confirm the news? If a company announces a massive order win but the stock opens gap down and forms a red candle, the market is rejecting the news. This is a massive red flag.
- **Check Insiders**: Are insiders or promoters buying or selling concurrent with positive news? Positive news used as exit liquidity for promoters is a classic trap.
- **Check Institutions**: Are institutional flows aligned? If retail sentiment is euphoric but FIIs are heavy sellers of that specific stock on the day of the news, trust the FIIs.
- **Apply Penalties**: If contradictions are found, apply a severe penalty to the signal score and lower conviction. It is better to miss a trade than to buy into a trap.
- **Anchoring Bias**: Don't over-weight the most recent news. Look at the broader trend. One good quarter does not erase a multi-year structural decline.
